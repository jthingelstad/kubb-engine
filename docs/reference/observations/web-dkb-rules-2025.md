# Offizielles Regelwerk des Deutschen Kubb-Bund e.V. (from 2025)

The German federation's rules, in force from its members' meeting of 2025. Seven pages
in eleven chapters. They differ from the U.S. rules in several places that bear on the
plays: a kubb must stand wholly inside, punishment kubbs are thrown rather than placed,
and kubbs lying on other kubbs are placed by the throwing team. Paraphrased in English
here; the quotation is the German, marked exact.
