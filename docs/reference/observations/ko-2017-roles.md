# New to Kubb: Team Roles

"New to Kubb: Team Roles", Kubb On, by Christopher Jones, dated June 28, 2017. A short
beginners' piece naming five roles on a kubb team. The author's note says he met kubb in
2013, played his first tournament in 2014, co-founded Kalamazoo Kubb and Kubb On, and
belongs to the St. Paul Kubb Society and Twin Cities Kubb clubs. The players in the
photographs are named in the captions only.
