# Tornooi- en wedstrijdregels, Kovera Kubb League (2026-1)

The Belgian federation's (Kubbfederatie) tournament and league rules, revision 2026-1 of
its 2025 rules, written with Belgian league teams. Terms, then the match rules in
numbered chapters. Paraphrased in English here; the quotation is the Dutch, marked exact.

| `p10.1` | the rules | rules | II.1: base kubbs stand against the baseline at least a baton length from the corner stakes, upright, and not with a corner toward the opponent; their back touches the baseline and they stand 100% in. | said |

| `p14.2` | the rules | field | II.4.1 and 4.2: a baton turning more than 30 degrees in the horizontal plane before it lands or hits is a helicopter throw and void; end-over-end rotation is allowed if it strays no more than 30 degrees left or right. | said |

| `p16.1` | the rules | field | II.4.3: for batons and kubbs alike, both feet wholly behind the baseline (or the line the player may advance to) and within the sidelines, and the thrower stays in that area until the pieces lie still. | said |

| `p18.2` | the rules | punish | II.5: a kubb still out after the two rounds is put by the opponent anywhere, a baton length from the king if behind it, and a baton length from the back line. | said |
| `p19.1` | the rules | raise | II.6: one player of the other team stands the kubbs. There are only two ways: up onto one end or the other, with at least one edge on the ground throughout; it is exact: "niet toegestaan om de kubb te draaien of te roteren" (not allowed to turn or rotate it) so as to change the direction or place it would stand in. The end is decided first, and the kubb moved as little as possible. | said |
| `p20.1` | the rules | raise | II.6: once raising has begun the decision cannot be reversed, except where the kubb would stand out. A raised kubb may be pressed firm but not hammered. | said |
| `p20.2` | the rules | raise | II.6: a kubb is in when, once stood, it is 100% inside the imaginary line along the outside of the stakes. | said |
| `p22.1` | the rules | raise | II.6: where the ground prevents a kubb standing stably, something such as a tuft of grass may be put under it, but not to make it lean one way or the other. | said |
