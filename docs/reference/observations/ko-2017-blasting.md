# Blasting: Finesse Style

"Blasting: Finesse Style", Kubb On, by Kyle Weakland, dated April 7, 2017. A first-person
article on how he throws a baton at a group of field kubbs. He says his team, the Kubb
Squirrels, made him its blaster about a month before the 2016 U.S. Kubb championship, and
that he built his method by studying YouTube videos of top teams. The author's note says
he met kubb in 2013, has played competitively since 2015, and co-founded the Burning River
Kubb Club and Kubb On. Other players named appear only in the clips he describes: Dave
Giese and Chris Hodges (Kubb United) and Emily Jipp (Kubbatrubbas).
