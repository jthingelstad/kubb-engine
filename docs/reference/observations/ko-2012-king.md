# Attacking the King

"Attacking the King", by Josh Feathers, dated February 22, 2012, filed under Strategy on
Kubb On and marked as reposted from the Des Moines Kubb Blog Archive. The author, who
says he misses the king often and leaves king shots to his partner, works through
whether to throw at the king from the corner of the baseline instead of its middle. It
rests on geometry he draws himself (pitch size, angles, the visible width of an Old Time
Games king) and on his feel for how much force a baton needs; he gives no working for the
surface figures and no count of shots. He ends undecided and still throws head-on.
