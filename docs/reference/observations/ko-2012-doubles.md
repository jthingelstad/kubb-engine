# Facing your Fears of Doubles and Triples

"Facing your Fears of Doubles and Triples", by Josh Feathers, dated May 11, 2012, filed
under Strategy on Kubb On and marked as reposted from the Des Moines Kubb Blog Archive.
It is a short piece on raising: how the facing of a standing kubb (which way its corner
or flat side points at the attacker) changes whether one baton can take two kubbs. It
rests on the author's own play and on three drawn diagrams; no count or test is given.
It links to his "Attacking the King" piece (`ko-2012-king`) for the facing argument, to
the Planet Kubb wiki's Fence page, and to the 2012 Kubbnation Magazine PDF.
