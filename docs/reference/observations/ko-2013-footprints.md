# On Footprints, Kubbadoxes, and the Golden Rules

"On Footprints, Kubbadoxes, and the Golden Rules", by Josh Feathers, dated March 6, 2013,
filed under Rules on Kubb On and marked as reposted from the Des Moines Kubb Blog
Archive (the original was posted the same day under "Advanced Rules", tagged "kubb
raising"). It sets out how a thrown kubb is raised: the footprint, two "Golden Rules" he
says are in the U.S. National Rules, three cases the rules cannot resolve, an order of
preference the rules mandate, and his own order for raising a crowded group. It rests on
the rules as he reads them and his experience raising tight groups; it quotes no rule
text and cites no section numbers.

For comparison with a rules implementation, the entries below keep each rule, case and
ruling apart: `p1.3` to `p1.5` the footprint and the in-bounds test, `p1.7` to `p1.9` the
two Golden Rules, `p1.15` to `p1.27` the three unresolvable cases and their rulings,
`p1.28` to `p1.30` the mandated preferences, and `p1.31` to `p1.39` his raising order,
which he says is best practice, not rule.
