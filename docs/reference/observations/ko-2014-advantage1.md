# Advantage, Efficiency, and Game Theory (Part 1 of 3)

The first of three posts by Chris Hodges of Des Moines Kubb, dated April 27, 2014, from
the Des Moines Kubb blog, reposted in Kubb On's archive of that blog. It sets out the
debate over the first team's head start (throwing first with six batons) and tests it
with a simulator of his own. His data, as he describes it: about 3,500 recorded matches
with results only; a little over 100 games recorded throw by throw (from Planet Kubb,
plus some videos he watched); and over 5,000 recorded throws at all distances, from
competitive play over the past few years. He names no teams in this part. It points
the reader to a Basel City Kubb post on the Basel-3 rule, an Ask Planet Kubb thread and a
Fox Valley Kubb Radio episode, and does not restate them.
