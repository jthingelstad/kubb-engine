# Ask a Kubb Ref: Field to Baseline Situations

"Ask a Kubb Ref: Field to Baseline Situations", Kubb On, by Jesse Fraim, dated February
9, 2023. Two readers' questions, each answered by the author: one on names for a throw
that takes base kubbs through a field kubb, one on standing up a base kubb knocked down too
early. The author's note says he started playing in 2013, has played tournaments in the
Midwest since 2014, co-founded Kalamazoo Kubb in late 2013 and is a Level 3 certified Kubb
United referee. The questioners are Rob House (Minneapolis) and Drew Gander (Raleigh,
North Carolina); Stu Gander and Sven Hickes of Tuesday Night Kubb supply joke names.

| `p1.7` | Jesse Fraim | rules | A base kubb knocked down too early goes back where it stood. If players disagree where that was, they agree a spot or ask a referee or tournament director. | said |
| `p1.8` | Jesse Fraim | rules | The baton, or any piece that came to rest near the base kubbs, is not removed; it stays to help or hinder the batons still to be thrown. | said |
