# The Answer

A post by Chris Hodges of Des Moines Kubb, dated July 26, 2012, from the Des Moines Kubb
blog, in Kubb On's archive. It is about the second team's first turn, which his club
calls "the Answer": how well Team B replies to Team A's opening. Its data: 32 games
recorded at PlanetKubb.com at the time (14 won by Team A, 18 by Team B), players and
events not named, plus one ranked match in Des Moines between two named local players.
