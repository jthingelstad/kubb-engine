# Beginner's Guide to Inkasting

"Beginner's Guide to Inkasting", Kubb On, by Kyle Weakland, dated February 19, 2018. A
short article introducing a video in which he shows how he learned to inkast; the page
itself gives a list of things to remember. He says he has taught many new players. The
author's note says he met kubb in 2013, has played competitively since 2015, and
co-founded the Burning River Kubb Club and Kubb On.
