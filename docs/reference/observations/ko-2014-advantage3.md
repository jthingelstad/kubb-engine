# Advantage, Efficiency, and Game Theory (Part 3 of 3)

The last of three posts by Chris Hodges of Des Moines Kubb, dated April 29, 2014, from
the Des Moines Kubb blog, in Kubb On's archive. It puts a number on the first team's head
start in the efficiency measure of part 2, estimates it under shorter openings from
simulation alone, gives his opinion on which opening to choose, and announces that Des
Moines Kubb will play a four-baton start in 2014. His basis: the game-level data of parts
1 and 2 (the 100-odd throw-by-throw games, competition level, the past few years), which
he says matches his simulation closely; for shorter openings, simulated games only. An
infographic, uncredited, gives average throws in championship matches.
