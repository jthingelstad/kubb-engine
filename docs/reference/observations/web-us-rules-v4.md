# U.S. National Kubb Championship Rules V4.0

The official rules of the U.S. National Kubb Championship, Eau Claire, Wisconsin,
adapted from the World Championship rules of 2003. Twelve pages: setup and overview,
the phases in depth, enforcement and penalties, and a glossary. No date is printed. These
are the rules the engine follows (docs/notes/rules-engine.md).

Only rule text that bears on a play or an open question is entered here. Quotations are a
few words, marked exact.
