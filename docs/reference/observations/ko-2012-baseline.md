# 42 – The answer to life, the universe, and baseline percentages

"42 – The answer to life, the universe, and baseline percentages", bylined Josh Feathers,
dated February 10, 2012, filed under Strategy on Kubb On with the Des Moines Kubb Blog
Archive footer. Most of it is excerpts from an email thread Feathers was copied on, in
which Chris Hodges argues from numbers. The excerpts speak in the first person of
someone who trained with Chris for Rockford; the page does not say whether that is
Feathers or another correspondent, so those rows are given to "Josh Feathers, relaying
Chris Hodges". The piece rests on two things: a hit rate of 42% put through the
probabilities of six throws, and Chris's review of Des Moines Kubb's video of ranked and
tournament games (number of games not given). It opens with a joking disclaimer that the
writers are enthusiasts with spreadsheets, not authorities. Its one image is a badge
reading "42", "TEAM ADAMS" and five stars, a nod to Douglas Adams, showing nothing about
play.
