# Strategy (Planet Kubb wiki)

The Planet Kubb wiki's "Strategy" page, revision 690, last modified 19 March 2012 (the
2016 snapshot gives 17:51, the 2012 one 16:51, by its time zone). Its talk page has one
note, dated 26 February 2012, from the wiki user Thingles (signed Jamie), saying he moved
this section from the Main Page to its own page so it could be expanded. No writer of
any passage is named. The page has five short sections (kubbs, batons, raising,
punishment kubb placement, rescuing), each a few general statements of what "players"
or "teams" do, with no examples, counts or sources beyond one appeal to the U.S. National
Championship Rules. The rescue section matches Josh Feathers' "Kubb Rescue Squad"
(`ko-2012-rescue`, 16 March 2012) closely in substance. It links to the wiki's page "The
hard way" (`pk-hardway`).
