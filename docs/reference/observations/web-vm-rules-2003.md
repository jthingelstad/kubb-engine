# Kubb, a game for all: World Championship Rules

The World Championship's English booklet: a translation of 1998 from the Swedish,
updated in 2003. Short rules on one page, then a sample game. It has no section numbers.
It is far less detailed than the U.S. rules adapted from it, and on raising it says less.

| `p4.3` | the booklet | baseline | Batons are thrown standing on or behind one's own baseline, both feet within the sidelines. | said |
