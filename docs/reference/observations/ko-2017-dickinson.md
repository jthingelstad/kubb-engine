# How I Practice: Phil Dickinson

"How I Practice: Phil Dickinson", Kubb On, by Phil Dickinson, dated May 16, 2017. He
describes how his club keeps practice fresh and then his five favourite solo practices,
each with a scoring rule and his own target. The author's note says he started playing in
2005, founded the Great Lakes Kubb Club, hosts the Michigan 1v1 Championship, the Great
Lakes Kubb Championship and the Great Lakes Kid Kubb Championship, and has made yard games
since 2001.
