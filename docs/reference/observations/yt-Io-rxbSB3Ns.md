## Caption problems

The auto captions never spell "kubb" right: in this one video it is koobs, coupe, cube,
coop, cool, Cleaves, tubes and ku-band. "Inkast" is "in cast" and batons are "times".
Anything quoted from captions needs the game's word list applied, and must say so.
