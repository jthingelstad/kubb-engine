# 1v1 Kubb World Championship 2025: recap and interviews

"1v1 Kubb World Championship 2025 Recap & Interviews", Kubb On, by Jason Larson, dated
November 3, 2025. The tournament was the 1v1 Kubb World Championship in Chaska, Minnesota
(not the U.S. national championship: Trevor Bailey lists the U.S. 1v1 Nationals as a
separate event). A short recap is followed by written answers from five players to the
same seven questions, each answer under the player's name. Most questions are about the
players' history and what playing alone is like; few answers say anything about how to
play. The author's note says he has played since 2007, makes kubb sets and teaches more
than he plays.

## The speakers

As the piece gives them.

- **Jason Larson**, the author: the recap paragraphs.
- **Barb Lancette**, Minneapolis: won the women's division, the first 1v1 Women's Kubb
  World Champion. This was her second 1v1.
- **Trevor Bailey**, Bloomington, Minnesota: won the men's division. Lists his own 1v1
  record (`p1.5`).
- **John Oman**: playing actively since 2012. His finish is not given. (Zach Oman, second
  in the men's division, is a different name.)
- **Jake Skerletts**: plays mostly team tournaments; his second 1v1. Finish not given.
- **J.J. Weigel**: Chaska Kubb Club; his third 1v1. Finish not given.
