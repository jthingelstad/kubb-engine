THE GAME'S WORDS. Spell them this way: kubb (plural kubbs), king, baton, inkast (throwing the kubbs into the other half), inkastare, drill (the spun inkast that lands nose down), cut, basket, field kubb (a kubb thrown into the field and raised), base kubb (a kubb on a baseline), baseline, centre line (or "center line"), sideline, corner stake, pin (the centre line's stake at a sideline), advantage line (the line a team throws from when the other side left field kubbs standing), raise or raising, punishment kubb, rethrow, blast, helicopter.

HOW TO MARK EVERY STATEMENT. Put one tag in square brackets at the start of each statement:

- [UNCLEAR] you cannot tell. Always say what hides it: the camera angle, a cut, the frame rate or motion blur, the edge of the frame, players standing in the way.
